# Frontend Design Specification

This document outlines the custom design standards, exact color palette, and typography rules for the application.

## 1. Color Palette

* **Background:** `#FCF6EE` (Warm, off-white/cream base)
* **Primary:** `#4E1FBE` (Deep vibrant purple used for primary buttons, headers, and major brand elements)
* **Secondary:** `#EAFF57` (Bright neon/lime yellow accent used for highlights, tags, and interactive focus states)
* **Tint-One:** `#E2D6FF` (Soft light purple tint used for cards, subtle backgrounds, and hover containers)
* **White:** `#FFFFFF` (Clean contrast surfaces)

## 2. Typography

* **Main Font:** Onsite font (applied globally across the entire website)
* **Font Weights:**
  * **Regular:** Used for body text, descriptions, and secondary paragraphs.
  * **Medium:** Used for form labels, subheadings, and secondary interactive buttons.
  * **Bold:** Used for hero titles, section headings, and primary calls to action.

## 3. Spacing & Layout Principles

* **Minimalist Aesthetics:** Clean whitespace, comfortable padding, and high contrast between the `#FCF6EE` background and `#4E1FBE` elements.
* **Component Styling:** Soft border radiuses, subtle shadows utilizing the tint variations, and clear visual hierarchy.
